About {{siteName}}

{{siteName}} is a small, independent project that makes it easy to design printable cards for the first months of life: birth announcements, monthly milestone cards, high-contrast flashcards for newborns, and baby shower games.

Why it exists

Most printable templates are static files that are hard to personalize. We wanted tools where you can see your own name, photo and numbers on the card straight away, then print it at home or at a print shop.

Privacy by design

Cards are generated in your browser. Photos you add are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. Read the privacy policy for details.
