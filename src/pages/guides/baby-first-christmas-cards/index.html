Baby's first Christmas & New Year cards

A first Christmas is a lovely reason for a keepsake, and many families send one card for the holidays and the new year together. Below are three free designs you can personalize in your browser, ideas for what to write, simple photo setups and a few print tips. Plan it early: babies are not always in the mood on the day.

Christmas · Little Animals Christmas · Classic Red New Year · Midnight Gold

Make a Christmas card

Tap a design to open it with your own details. Add a photo, then choose the greeting: Baby's First Christmas, Merry Christmas, Happy New Year, Merry Christmas & Happy New Year or Season's Greetings. The Little Animals design lets you pick the animal in the Santa hat. Everything runs in your browser, so your photos stay on your device.

What to write

Keep it short. The photo and the name do most of the work.

  • "Our first Christmas as a family of three."
  • "Baby's first Christmas" with the year and the baby's age, for example "3 months old".
  • "Warm wishes from our little family."
  • "The best gift arrived early this year."
  • "Merry Christmas from the newest little elf."
  • "Thank you for all your love in our baby's first year."
  • "Wishing you a happy, healthy new year."
  • "New year, new little person, so much love."
  • Weight and length, as a snapshot of how small the baby still is.
  • One line about this year: the first smile, the first time the baby saw the tree lights.

Sign it with both parents' names. In the card maker, type them under Mother and Father and they appear as "with love, Emma & Mark". Use the short message line for one of the wishes above.

Photo setups that stay simple

  • The blanket shot: baby on a plain blanket by a window, with a few lights in the background.
  • The tree: sit near the tree and let the lights blur behind. Turn off the room lights and use only the tree for a warm glow.
  • The outfit: a simple red or green romper, a knitted hat or a small pair of socks.
  • The family: one photo with hands only, holding the baby's hand, needs no perfect smile.

Take photos a few days before you need the card, once the baby is fed and rested, and keep each session short. A round photo frame suits these designs, so leave a little space around the baby's face.

A card for the new year: baby's year in review

New Year is a good moment to look back. Two simple ideas:

  • A New Year card: choose the New Year · Midnight Gold design. Without a photo it shows the new year in big gold numbers; with a photo, the year sits softly behind it.
  • A year-in-review card: use the milestone card maker with Write my own, for example big text "2026" and small text "OUR FIRST YEAR", then list a favorite thing, a new skill and a fun fact from the year.

Print and send on time

  • Early December: take the photo and make the card.
  • Print at home: choose Print at home (PDF) and use card stock of 200 to 250 gsm.
  • Print shop: choose Print shop quality (PDF), which adds a 3 mm cutting margin, and order at least a week before you post.
  • Send by phone: choose Picture to share (PNG) for WhatsApp or messages, perfect for family far away.

Questions

Are the Christmas cards free?

Yes. Make and download as many as you like, with no sign-up.

Can I make one card for Christmas and New Year?

Yes. Choose the greeting Merry Christmas & Happy New Year on any of the three designs.

Which year does the card show?

Christmas greetings show this year. Happy New Year shows the coming year from October onwards, so a card made in December says the right year.

Can I make a card without a photo?

Yes. Switch off Show photo in the Photo section: the Little Animals design shows the animal by the tree, Classic Red shows a tree inside the wreath and the New Year design shows the year in gold.

What size should I print?

A5 suits a card in an envelope. A4 or US Letter makes a small poster for the fridge or a baby book.
