What to write in a baby shower card: 70 message ideas

A baby shower card does not need to be long. Two or three warm lines, a wish for the baby and your name are plenty. Below are messages for every kind of sender: close friends, coworkers, grandparents, first-time parents and families welcoming another little one. Pick one, make it personal with a name or a memory, and sign off.

A simple formula

  1. Congratulate: "Congratulations on your little one!"
  2. Add something personal: a memory, a quality you admire, or how excited you are.
  3. Wish them well: health, sleep, joy, an easy start.
  4. Sign off: "With love", "Hugs", "Can't wait to meet the baby".

Advice ideas for the parents-to-be

Many showers have an advice card game: every guest writes a tip for the new parents. If you are stuck, try one of these:

  • "Sleep when the baby sleeps, and leave the dishes."
  • "Say yes when people offer to help, especially with meals."
  • "Take a photo of the baby next to the same teddy every month."
  • "Write down the funny moments. You'll forget them faster than you think."
  • "It's a phase. The hard ones pass, so enjoy the sweet ones."
  • "Trust your instincts. You know your baby best."
  • "Keep a spare outfit in every bag. For you, too."
  • "Go outside every day, even for ten minutes."

Questions

How long should a baby shower card message be?

Two to four sentences is perfect. Congratulate, add one personal line, wish them well and sign off.

Do I write in the card if I'm giving a gift?

Yes. A short message makes the gift personal, and many parents keep the cards in the baby book.

What do I write if I don't know the parents well?

Keep it warm and simple: "Congratulations on your little one! Wishing you a healthy baby and a happy new chapter."

Should I mention the baby's name?

Only if the parents have shared it. If not, "little one" or "your baby" works well.
