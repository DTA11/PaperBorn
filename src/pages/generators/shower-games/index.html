Make your shower games in four steps

  1. Pick a game. Gift bingo, baby predictions or advice cards.
  2. Make it yours. Add a title and the guest of honour’s names. For bingo, type your own items, one per line (at least 24).
  3. Choose how many sheets. Every bingo sheet is shuffled so boards differ. Predictions and advice can fit 1, 2 or 4 cards per sheet.
  4. Download the PDF and print. Choose the Mono Line theme to save ink.

How to play

  • Gift bingo: guests mark each gift as it is opened. First to get five in a row calls bingo.
  • Predictions: everyone guesses the birth date, weight and more. Keep the cards and see who was closest.
  • Advice cards: guests write a tip or wish. Collect them in a keepsake box or book.

Frequently asked questions

Are the bingo boards really different?

Yes. Each sheet shuffles your items with its own number, so boards differ. Add more than 24 items for the most variety.

Can I change the centre square?

Yes. Change the FREE text to anything you like, such as “BABY”.

Is it free?

Yes. Make and print as many sets as you need.
