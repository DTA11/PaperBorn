Make a milestone card in four steps

  1. Choose the milestone. Newborn, 1–12 months, 18 months, 2 or 3 years, or type your own (for example “100 days”).
  2. Add this month’s details. Date, weight in grams, height in centimeters, a short note and three favorites.
  3. Add a photo and pick one of five designs: classic, chalkboard, pastel sticker, scrapbook or modern.
  4. Download a PNG or PDF, or build the whole first-year set in Monthly Set in One PDF.

Tips for great milestone photos

  • Take the photo in the same spot each month so growth is easy to see.
  • Use natural window light and a plain blanket or backdrop.
  • For the full set, switch off Date, Weight and Height first, then fill them in by hand after printing.
  • Print on matte paper of 170–250 gsm for a keepsake feel.
