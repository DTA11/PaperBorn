Make a baby stats card in four steps

  1. Pick a design. Twenty styles, from illustrated cards with cute animals, balloons and bows, Christmas and New Year cards to a magazine cover, a newspaper front page, a moon-phase night sky and a classic birth certificate.
  2. Add the details. Choose the birth date from the calendar and the zodiac sign is filled in for you. Weight is entered in grams and height in centimeters.
  3. Add a photo. Choose a frame shape and a color filter (black and white, sepia, warm and more) and adjust brightness, contrast and zoom.
  4. Download. Save a PDF for printing or a PNG for sharing. Pick A4, A5, US Letter, 8×10 or square.

Tips for a beautiful print

  • Print on matte or lightly textured paper of 170–250 gsm. It hides fingerprints and gives a gallery look.
  • Use the 8×10 Frame size if you plan to put the card in a standard frame, or A4 for a poster.
  • Sending it to a print shop? Choose Print shop quality when you download and turn on Cutting guides.
  • Choose the black-and-white photo filter for a timeless look, or natural color for bright, cheerful designs.
  • Do a draft print on plain paper first to check the margins and text size.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. You can design and download as many cards as you like.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The card is built in your browser. Your photo never leaves your device.

How is the zodiac sign calculated?

From the birth date you choose, using the standard Western tropical zodiac date ranges.

What does the moon phase show?

An approximation of the moon's phase on the birth date (accurate to about a day). It appears on the Celestial Night and Cyber designs when "Moon phase" is switched on.

Why do I see my last card when I come back?

Your details, settings and photo are saved in your browser so you can continue later. Tap Start over to clear them.

Can I make cards for each month too?

Yes, try the monthly milestone card maker.
