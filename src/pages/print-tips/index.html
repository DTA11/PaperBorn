How to print baby cards at home

A few small choices make the difference between a card that looks homemade and one that looks professional. These tips apply to every card made with our baby stats, milestone, first birthday, flashcard and shower game makers.

Quick checklist

  • Download a PDF for printing (the picture is for phones).
  • Printer paper size = the size you chose in the maker.
  • Scale 100% / Actual size.
  • Highest quality, and the paper type that matches your paper.
  • Folded cards: two-sided, flip on short edge.
  • One test print on plain paper first.

PDF or picture?

Choose one of the PDF options for printing. A PDF keeps the paper size exact, so nothing is scaled. The picture (PNG) is for sharing on your phone, because it has no paper size.

Which download should I choose?

When you tap Download you can choose between three options:

  • Print at home (PDF) is a document for your own printer. The file is small.
  • Print shop quality (PDF) has the highest quality (about 300 dpi) and a 3 mm cutting margin (bleed), so it suits print shops and photo labs.
  • Picture to share (PNG) is an image for WhatsApp, messages or social media.
  • Folded greeting card (PDF) is one A4 or US Letter sheet, printed on both sides and folded in half (birth cards, birthday invitations and thank-you cards). See below.

Both PDFs contain the card as a high-resolution image in standard RGB color. They are not professional CMYK files with embedded fonts. Most photo labs and online print services accept them without trouble, but colors can shift a little when the shop converts them to its printer's inks. Ask for a test print if exact color matters.

Choose the paper

  • Everyday cards: 160-200 gsm matte card stock feels sturdy and hides fingerprints.
  • Photo cards: a satin or silk photo paper gives richer color, but pick a matte finish if the card will be handled a lot.
  • Shower games: normal 100 gsm paper is fine and costs the least.
  • Flashcards for newborns: thick card stock lasts longer. Laminating is optional.

Set the printer correctly

  • Choose the same paper size as in the generator (A4, A5 or US Letter).
  • In the print dialog set scaling to 100% or Actual size, not Fit to page.
  • Pick the highest quality and the paper type that matches your paper.
  • Print a test page on plain paper first to check the margins.

How to print a folded greeting card

The folded card PDF has two pages. Page 1 is the outside: the back on the left and your design as the front on the right. Page 2 is the inside: an empty left side and your message on the right.

  1. Open the PDF and choose Print. Paper A4 or US Letter, landscape.
  2. Turn on two-sided (duplex) printing and choose flip on short edge (some printers call it short-edge binding or flip up). This matters: with long edge the inside of the card comes out upside down.
  3. Scale 100% / Actual size. Use Fit to page only if your paper is a different size than the PDF (for example an A4 file on US Letter paper).
  4. Fold along the small marks at the top and bottom edge, with the design facing out. Crease the fold with the back of a spoon or a ruler.

No two-sided printing?

  1. Print only page 1.
  2. Put the printed sheet back in the paper tray so the empty side will be printed. Which way up depends on the printer: do it once on plain paper with an arrow drawn at the top edge, then you know.
  3. Print only page 2.

Best paper for folded cards

  • 200–250 gsm matte card stock: folds cleanly and feels like a shop card. Check that your printer takes this weight (often through the rear or manual feed).
  • Thicker than 250 gsm: score the fold first with a ruler and a blunt knife or an empty ballpoint pen, then fold.
  • Plain 80–100 gsm paper: fine for a test, but the outside shows through on the inside.

Printing from your phone

  • iPhone and iPad: download the PDF, open it, tap the share button and choose Print. Under the printer options turn on Double-sided for folded cards. Most Wi-Fi printers work with AirPrint.
  • Android: open the PDF, tap the menu and choose Print, then pick your printer. Two-sided printing is under More options or the printer's own settings.
  • If your phone cannot print two-sided, send the PDF to a computer, or use the one-side-at-a-time steps above.

When to use the bleed option

Cards with a colored background run to the edge of the paper. Home printers cannot print that far, so a white border appears. If you print at a print shop, choose Print shop quality, which adds a 3 mm margin so the color extends past the cutting line. At home, print on a larger sheet and trim.

Cutting and finishing

  • A paper trimmer gives straighter edges than scissors.
  • Round the corners with a corner punch for a softer, finished look.
  • Store milestone cards in a small box or a photo album sleeve.

Something looks wrong?

  • The inside of the folded card is upside down: print again with flip on short edge instead of long edge.
  • Edges are cut off or the card is smaller than expected: set the scale to 100% / Actual size and check the paper size.
  • A white border around a colored card: normal for home printers. Choose Print shop quality and trim, or print at a shop.
  • Colors look dull or dark: choose the highest quality and the right paper type (photo paper or matte) in the printer settings.
  • Lines or stripes on the print: run the printer's head cleaning from its settings, then print again.
  • The photo spot is empty: add a photo, or switch off Show photo before downloading.

Print shops

Most print shops accept a PDF. Tell them the paper weight and size, ask for no scaling, and request a color proof if you need many copies.
